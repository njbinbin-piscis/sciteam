# Skill: ideation.tension_hunt

## Purpose
Find research opportunities by locating real tensions between widely accepted
desiderata (performance↔efficiency, generality↔specialization, …).

## Provenance
Distilled from Orchestra Research ideation “Tension and Contradiction Hunting”
and creative-thinking practices. Adapted to SciTeam claim/hypothesis discipline.

## Requires capabilities
- literature.search
- literature.audit

## Inputs
- Field keywords; optional lit_map

## Outputs
- `tensions[]`: pair, evidence that each side is pursued, artifact-or-fundamental
  judgment, proposed reconciliation (or Pareto characterization), grounding refs

## Steps
1. List top desiderata in the mission domain (from tool-backed survey, not memory).
2. Pair trade-offs that the literature treats as opposing.
3. For each pair: cite work optimizing each side; ask whether the trade-off is
   fundamental or method-artifact.
4. Propose a falsifiable reconciliation or a frontier-characterization study.
5. Quarantine any citation that fails audit.

## Failure modes
- Assumed tensions with no literature; aspirational reconciliations with no eval hook.

## Stop
≥1 actionable tension with grounding and a falsification path.
