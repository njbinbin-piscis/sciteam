# Question (X-cog-0 smoke 4; not scored)

For a positive integer n, let f(n) be the number of subsets S of {1, 2, ..., n} (including the
empty set) whose element sum is divisible by n.

Determine f(n) for every positive integer n, as a closed form (sums over divisors are allowed),
with a complete proof that covers every n, even and odd. The final answer goes in
results/goal.md.
